Accessibility statement

Last updated: [launch date]

I want every business owner to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology.

The standard we follow

This site is designed and tested to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. In practice that means:

  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 against its background
  • Visible focus states and full keyboard access for menus, forms and accordions
  • Tap targets of at least 44 × 44 pixels
  • Headings in a logical order and descriptive link text
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Form fields with visible labels and clear error messages
  • Animation turned off when your device asks for reduced motion

Known limitations

Third-party tools, such as the booking calendar, are provided by other companies. We choose tools with good accessibility, but can’t fully control them. If one gets in your way, contact me and I’ll book the call for you directly.
