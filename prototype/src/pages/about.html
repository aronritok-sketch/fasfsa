02 Florida

A European perspective, built for Florida

[Verified: when and why you moved to Florida, and what you’ve built here since.]

Working across markets taught me something useful: the fundamentals of growth travel well, but the details never do. How a homeowner in Naples chooses a contractor is different from how a business buyer in Budapest chooses a partner. Good strategy starts with that local reality — who your customers are, how they search, and what makes them trust someone.

Southwest Florida is a remarkable place to build a business: fast population growth, seasonal rhythms, a premium market and fierce local competition. It rewards businesses that are easy to find, easy to trust and easy to contact. That’s exactly what I help build.

03 Principles

How I think about growth

  • Strategy means choosing.

    If everything is a priority, nothing is. Good strategy says what to do first — and what not to do yet.

  • Numbers need context.

    I start from data, but a metric rarely explains itself. I connect the numbers to your customers, your team and your decisions.

  • A plan you can execute beats a perfect plan.

    Recommendations have to fit your budget, your people and your pace.

  • Technology is a tool, not a strategy.

    Websites, SEO, ads and AI matter only when they solve a clear business problem.

04 Focus

What I focus on

My core work is digital growth strategy for established Florida businesses, supported by three disciplines: strategic website design and development, local SEO and search growth, and conversion-focused digital marketing. When something falls outside what I do best, I’ll tell you — and point you to someone who does it better.
